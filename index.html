Guided setup

Get the workbook ready

Follow the steps below. Advanced settings can be left unchanged unless you know they apply.

    What do these terms mean?
    Common part
    A part appearing in more than one source sheet. The largest required quantity is used.
    Landed cost
    The purchase price plus freight, insurance, customs duty and clearance costs.
    Margin
    The amount left after costs. VAT is not counted as income or profit.
    Shipment split
    How one shared freight invoice is divided between parts: by purchase value, quantity or number of lines.
    Stock turns
    How many times the current stock quantity is expected to sell during one year.

    VAT by sales country

    This project's routes

    VAT is added to the price after your margin, never to the purchase cost. By default sales to Norwegian customers carry no VAT (goods from the USA or from a Swedish warehouse), while sales to Swedish customers do (Swedish stock, goods from Norway where VAT is paid when you sell, and goods bought from Switzerland). VAT is shown separately and never counted as profit or cost.
